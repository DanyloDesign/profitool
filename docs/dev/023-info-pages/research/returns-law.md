## 2. Distance purchase, no defect (withdrawal)

Current law (1023-XII), art. 13 "Право споживача у разі укладення договору на відстані", plus art. 13 pt 7 which pulls in art. 12 pts 5-9. https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1023-12
- Period and start: art. 13 pt 4: consumer may terminate by notifying the seller "протягом чотирнадцяти днів з моменту підтвердження інформації або з моменту одержання товару чи першої поставки товару". If the confirmation of information does not meet art. 13 pt 3, the period is 90 days from receipt of the goods (pt 4 para 4). For goods the return itself also counts as termination (pt 4 para 3).
- Buyer must: notify within 14 days; keep the goods "у незмінному стані" (art. 12 pt 7). Opening the package, inspecting or testing the goods and any resulting loss of value does not remove the right (art. 12 pt 7, via art. 13 pt 7). Destruction or damage not caused by the buyer does not remove it either.
- Who pays return shipping: the SELLER. Art. 12 pt 5 (applies via art. 13 pt 7): "Будь-які витрати, пов'язані з поверненням продукції, покладаються на продавця (виконавця). Продавець повинен відшкодувати витрати споживача у зв'язку з поверненням продукції." The buyer tells the seller where goods can be returned; the contract may say goods sent by post come back by post.
- Refund deadline: art. 12 pt 4: "без затримки не пізніше тридцяти днів з моменту повідомлення споживачем про розірвання договору". The counting starts at the buyer's NOTICE, not at receipt of the goods. The buyer "має право не повертати продукцію ... до моменту повернення йому сплаченої ним суми", so the seller cannot lawfully hold the refund until the goods arrive.
- Late refund: penalty 1% of the product price per day (art. 12 pt 9).
- Seller storage: buyer's duty to keep the goods ends 60 days after receipt if the seller does not collect; the goods then pass to the buyer (art. 12 pt 5).
- Diminished value: no deduction for opening or inspection (art. 12 pt 7). Deduction for use beyond inspection: the 1991 law has no explicit rule. UNVERIFIED whether a seller can lawfully deduct; art. 12 pt 7 requires the goods to stay "у незміненому стані".
- Pre-contract duty: art. 13 pt 2 (see section 6).

New law (3153-IX, not in force), art. 21 https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/3153-20 :
- pt 1: 14 days from conclusion of contract, or from receipt of goods by the consumer or a third party, or from the first delivery. Excludes goods in art. 5 pt 5.
- pt 3 and pt 6: the consumer only has to send the notice before the 14 days end. Pt 7: the burden of proving the notice is on the consumer.
- pt 4: seller refunds within 14 days "з дня отримання товару від споживача", including delivery to the consumer if the contract says so; extra cost of a delivery faster or dearer than the cheapest option offered is not refunded. Art. 20 pt 6: refund by the same method as payment unless the consumer agrees otherwise.
- pt 8: return at the CONSUMER's expense, except (1) seller offers to collect at its own cost, (2) seller did not state the return method and costs under art. 17 pt 1 sub-pt 9. Consumer returns the goods within 14 days from notifying the seller (pt 8, art. 22 pt 4). Consumer is liable only for loss of value from handling "відмінного від призначення товару чи вимог інструкції"; no liability if the seller omitted the withdrawal notice.
- pt 5: if the seller fails to give the withdrawal information (art. 17 pt 1 sub-pt 9), the period becomes 12 months.
- Min. of Economy order No. 26021 approves a sample withdrawal form (pt 2: the website must show the form and instructions; if the consumer files via the site the seller must confirm receipt "невідкладно").

CONFLICT BETWEEN REGIMES (decision for the owner): return shipping is paid by the seller now (art. 12 pt 5) and by the consumer after the new law (art. 21 pt 8), and the refund clock runs from the notice now (30 days) and from receipt of the goods under the new law (14 days).

## 3. Exceptions (goods that cannot be returned)

- Distance contracts, current law, art. 13 pt 5 (unless the contract says otherwise): electronic delivery before the period ends with consent; price tied to financial-market quotes; made-to-order or customised goods; opened sealed audio/video media or software; periodicals; lotteries and gambling. Power tools, discs, bits, helmets are NOT on this list. https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1023-12
- Art. 13 pt 1 excludes real estate, securities, financial services, vending machine sales, e-communications, auctions, payment services from art. 13 altogether.
- KMU resolution of 19.03.1994 No. 172, annex 3 (list of quality goods not subject to exchange/return, authorised by art. 9 pt 1, last edit 07.12.2005, valid as of 17.10.2026): food and medicines, hygiene items, perfumery, gloves, bedding, underwear, aerosols, printed matter, discs/cassettes with recordings, shaving items, manicure tools, jewellery, and "лінійний та листковий металопрокат, трубна продукція, пиломатеріали, погонажні, плитні матеріали і скло, нарізані або розкроєні під розмір, визначений покупцем". No power tools, accessories or helmets. Rolled metal/boards cut to a size the buyer chose would be excluded if the store ever sells cut-to-size items. https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/172-94-%D0%BF
- The annex 3 list belongs to the in-store exchange right (art. 9). Whether it also limits distance withdrawal under art. 13 is not stated in art. 13, whose own exception list is in the first bullet above. I read it as not applying. UNVERIFIED by a court or Держпродспоживслужба source.
- No exception for "technically complex" goods in either the current law or the new law's return provisions. The "технічно складні побутові товари" concept (art. 1 pt 25-1, art. 6, art. 8 pt 11 of the 1991 law) governs warranty and receipts only.
- New law, art. 5 pt 5 (not in force): not returnable are (1) made to individual order, (2) perishable, (3) packaged goods unpacked such that hygiene or health properties are affected, (4) goods mixed or inseparably joined with other items, (5) printed media, (6) auction goods, (7) opened packaged audio/video/software, (8) started digital content, (9) goods damaged through the consumer's fault, (10) goods on the KMU list. Item 9 and the "unused" wording make use a ground to refuse. Opened packaging is a ground only for hygiene goods (item 3). https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/3153-20
- Opening versus use: current law, art. 12 pt 7 allows opening and inspection. Test-running a tool to check it works is inspection; I found no definition of where inspection ends and use begins (UNVERIFIED).

## 4. Purchase in a physical store (pickup or in-store)

- Current law art. 9 pt 1: right to EXCHANGE a non-food quality item for an analogue from the same seller "протягом чотирнадцяти днів, не рахуючи дня купівлі, якщо триваліший строк не оголошений продавцем", if it does not suit "за формою, габаритами, фасоном, кольором, розміром або з інших причин". Conditions: unused, presentation, consumer properties, seals, labels, and the receipt (or QR/electronic receipt) preserved. Goods on the KMU list are excluded. https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1023-12
- Art. 9 pt 2: if no analogue is in stock, the buyer may take other goods with a price adjustment, terminate the contract and get the money back, or wait for the analogue to arrive.
- Art. 9 pt 3: refund based on the price at purchase, "у день розірвання договору", and if impossible that day "не пізніше ніж протягом семи днів".
- New law art. 5 pt 4 (not in force): right to exchange OR return a quality item within 14 days "з дня його отримання"; same conditions (unused, presentation, seals, receipt) and refund by the original payment method. Art. 5 pt 7: refund the same day or within 7 days. https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/3153-20 . Bill 15100 would make this right conditional on the seller offering it (see section 1).
- Important for the store: an order placed on the website and picked up in Sofiivka is still a distance contract (art. 1 pt 8: contract concluded by distance means). That means the art. 13 withdrawal regime applies, not only art. 9. UNVERIFIED by a regulator source for the pickup case.

## 5. Defective item (неналежна якість)

Current law, art. 8 and art. 6 https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1023-12 :
- Remedies during the warranty period (art. 8 pt 1): proportional price reduction; free repair within a reasonable time; reimbursement of repair costs. For "істотні недоліки, які виникли з вини виробника (продавця)" or counterfeits, confirmed by expert opinion if needed, the buyer may choose a refund or replacement with the same or an analogous item.
- Order: the buyer may press one of the pt 1 demands and, if it is not met, another (art. 8 pt 3 para 2).
- Claim addressee (art. 8 pt 3): at the buyer's choice, the seller at the place of purchase, the manufacturer, or a service enterprise near the buyer.
- Deadlines: repair "протягом чотирнадцяти днів з дати його пред'явлення або за згодою сторін в інший строк" (art. 8 pt 9); penalty 1% of the price per day of delay; a loan unit of an analogous model on written request (art. 8 pt 9, exchange fund per KMU No. 172 annex 2, which lists household electrics and does not name power tools). Replacement if the item is in stock: immediately, or within 14 days if quality needs checking (art. 8 pt 6); if not in stock, within two months. Refund: on the day of termination, or within 7 days (art. 8 pt 7).
- After the warranty (art. 8 pt 10): a free-repair demand is possible during the service life, or within 10 years if none is set, for defects the manufacturer caused. If not met within 14 days, other pt 1 remedies open.
- Shipping to service (art. 8 pt 5): the seller or manufacturer must accept the defective item; delivery of "великогабаритних товарів і товарів вагою понад п'ять кілограмів" to the seller/manufacturer and back is at THEIR cost. For lighter items the 1991 law is silent on who pays delivery (UNVERIFIED). Practice and the new law both put it on the seller (below).
- Documents (art. 8 pt 11): claims are considered after the buyer presents the receipt, and for goods with a warranty the technical passport or equivalent with the date of sale. Lost passport: restored per the legal procedure. For "технічно складні побутові товари" the fiscal receipt and passport are both required. Lost receipt: the law says claims are considered after presenting it; I found no source saying the claim is barred outright without it, and proof by other means is a general civil-law question (UNVERIFIED).
- Seller defence (art. 8 pt 14): not liable if it proves the defect came from the consumer's misuse or storage; the consumer may attend the quality check.
- Technically complex goods: art. 1 pt 25-1 defines them; the list of groups and the repair procedure are set by KMU resolution of 28.11.2023 No. 1251 (valid as of 17.10.2026). Its list of groups includes "Машини та прилади електропобутові", "Машини та прилади для механізації робіт у побуті" and "Продукція виробничо-технічного призначення, яка може бути використана у побуті". Power tools are not named; whether a drill falls under these groups is UNVERIFIED. The procedure requires acceptance of the item with a receipt/passport, a repair receipt stating the service centre, and notice of completion (items 5-8). https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1251-2023-%D0%BF . Note: No. 1251 cites "частини дванадцятої статті 7" of the law, which is the NEW law's numbering. Whether it already applies is UNVERIFIED.

New law, art. 7 (not in force) https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/3153-20 :
- pt 1: first remedies are warranty repair or replacement. Pt 2: free of charge, within 14 days of the claim or another agreed term, without significant inconvenience.
- pt 5: price reduction or withdrawal if repair or replacement is not done, the same defect reappears, the defect is serious, repair/replacement cannot be done in time, or the seller says it is uneconomical.
- pt 7: withdrawal by written or electronic statement. Pt 8: the consumer returns the item and the seller refunds after receiving the item or a copy of the shipping document (text cut at the end in my read: UNVERIFIED for the exact deadline). Pt 9: refund the same day or within 7 days.
- pt 12: receipt plus passport/equivalent required. Pt 13: "Доставка суб'єкту господарювання товару з недоліком та повернення відремонтованого або заміненого товару споживачу здійснюються за рахунок суб'єкта господарювання" for ALL goods, no weight limit.

## 6. Information the seller must publish (distance sales)

Current law, art. 13 pt 2: before the contract, the seller gives (1) name, address and the procedure for accepting claims, (2) main characteristics, (3) price including delivery and payment terms, (4) warranty obligations and after-sales service, (5) other delivery terms, (6) minimum duration for recurring supply, (7) e-communications cost, (8) offer validity period, (9) procedure for terminating the contract. Pt 3: confirm it in writing or by electronic message; it may not be changed unilaterally. Breach: liability under arts. 15 and 23. Pt 6: deliver within a reasonable term, at most 30 days from the consumer's consent. https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1023-12
- No statutory withdrawal form in the 1991 law.
- Art. 12 pt 2 content for off-premises sales is separate and does not apply to online shops.
- Law on e-commerce (Закон "Про електронну комерцію", 675-VIII) art. 7 requires seller identification: full name, address, registration data, tax ID (the new law's amendments restate it). Not read in full: UNVERIFIED. https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/675-19

New law, art. 17 pt 1 (not in force): the seller gives, as part of the contract, items including: goods name and properties; full legal name, registered and actual address, EDRPOU and VAT number (legal entity) or name, address and tax ID (FOP); phone, e-mail; the entity responsible for warranty claims; price and taxes; delivery costs; payment and delivery terms; complaint procedure; "порядок та умови реалізації права споживача на відмову від договору та повернення товару ... порядок та спосіб повернення коштів ... можливі витрати споживача, пов'язані з поверненням"; warranty and commercial guarantee; after-sales service; out-of-court dispute mechanism (18 items). Art. 21 pt 2: publish the approved withdrawal form and instructions on the website. https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/3153-20

## 8. Conflicts with the store's current terms

Store terms: return within 14 days if unused, packaging and receipt kept; return by Nova Poshta or in the Sofiivka store; refund no later than 7 days after the store receives the item for checking, same payment method; manufacturer warranty 12/24/36 months; own service in Sofiivka.

1. "Unused" for ONLINE orders. Under the current law (art. 13 pt 7 + art. 12 pt 7) the buyer may open and inspect, and the seller may not refuse for diminished value from inspection. "Unused" is lawful for the in-store exchange (art. 9 pt 1) but too strict as a blanket rule for distance orders. Suggest: "not used beyond what is needed to check it works"; allow opened packaging.
2. Start of the 14 days. The law counts from receipt of the goods (art. 13 pt 4), not from purchase or order. Terms must say "from the day you receive the order".
3. Notice versus return. The buyer only has to notify within 14 days; the return may follow. A rule requiring the item to arrive within 14 days would be stricter than the current law (art. 13 pt 4) and the new law (art. 21 pt 6, art. 22 pt 4, return within 14 days of notice).
4. Return shipping cost. Current law: seller pays (art. 12 pt 5). Terms do not say who pays. If the terms put Nova Poshta costs on the buyer, that conflicts with art. 12 pt 5 for as long as the 1991 law applies, and would match the new law only from its entry into force (art. 21 pt 8). Owner decision needed: pay return shipping now, or take the legal risk.
5. Refund deadline. Store: 7 days after receipt for checking. Current law: up to 30 days from the buyer's notice, and the seller cannot make the refund depend on receiving the goods (art. 12 pt 4). The store's 7 days after receipt is lawful only if the notice came at the same time as the shipment; a gap between notice and arrival that makes the total exceed 30 days breaches the law. A "checking" condition is not recognised for distance withdrawal. For in-store exchange refunds the limit is the same day or 7 days (art. 9 pt 3). Under the new law 14 days from receipt. A 7-day promise is stricter than both, which is lawful.
6. Refund method. Same payment method is required by the new law (art. 20 pt 6, art. 5 pt 4) and consistent with the current law (the current text says "повернути сплачені гроші"). No conflict.
7. Receipt as a condition. For defect claims the receipt and passport are required (art. 8 pt 11), so the condition is lawful. For distance withdrawal, a receipt is not named as a condition in art. 13; making it mandatory is risky (UNVERIFIED). Order confirmation should suffice.
8. Packaging. The law does not require original packaging for distance withdrawal in the 1991 law. For in-store exchange it requires "товарний вигляд, ярлики, пломби", not the box (art. 9 pt 1). New law art. 5 pt 4 keeps this. "Packaging kept" is acceptable as a request but cannot be an absolute ground for refusal for distance orders.
9. 12/24/36 months warranty: lawful, but repair deadline is 14 days with a 1% per day penalty (art. 8 pt 9). Shipping to/from service: seller pays for items over 5 kg, and for all items under the new law. Own service in Sofiivka is fine, but the buyer may choose to claim from the manufacturer or a nearby service (art. 8 pt 3).
10. Required notices missing from the terms: legal entity or FOP identification, procedure for accepting claims, withdrawal procedure (art. 13 pt 2), delivery costs (pt 2 sub-pt 3). Add a withdrawal form now to be ready for art. 21 pt 2 of the new law.
11. Exclusions. The store can list only made-to-order goods and sealed media among distance exceptions (art. 13 pt 5). Excluding opened power tools, discs, bits or helmets would not be supported by the 1991 law. Helmets sold for hygiene reasons: no hygiene exception exists in the current law for them (the KMU list names gloves and linen, not helmets): UNVERIFIED; the new law adds "unpacked hygiene-sensitive goods" (art. 5 pt 5 item 3).

Unverified summary: lost-receipt effect; deduction for use beyond inspection; whether pickup orders count as distance contracts; whether power tools fall under No. 1251's groups; bill 15100 status after 31.03.2026; e-commerce law text; order 26021 text; the weight-limit rule for shipping to service of lighter items under the 1991 law.
