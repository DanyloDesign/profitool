# Returns practice, Ukrainian retailers (checked 2026-10-17)

Method: WebFetch of official pages; where a fetch failed, the fact comes from a web-search snippet and is marked (SNIPPET). Anything not on the page is marked "not stated". Law is out of scope.

## Rozetka (own-seller goods, not marketplace)
Pages: https://help.rozetka.com.ua/p/176-yak-povernuti-tovar-prodavtsya-rozetka-noviy/ ; https://help.rozetka.com.ua/p/77-yaka-vartist-dostavky-tovaru-pry-povernenni/ ; https://help.rozetka.com.ua/p/76-u-yakyh-sytuaciyah-ya-mozhu-povernuty-tovar/ . (https://rozetka.com.ua/ua/news/obmin-ta-povernennya-tovaru/ returned 403: UNVERIFIED.)
- Period: 14 days from purchase (30 for clothing/footwear). RozetkaEU items: 30 days from parcel delivery (60 clothing).
- Conditions: new, unused, original packaging, full set (incl. gift), receipt/waybill, warranty card. Phones/laptops: sign out of accounts. Apple only if never activated. Water/gas-connected items only if never installed.
- Start: personal account > Orders > open order > return option > pick item and serial number > fill fields > save. Paper form also accepted. Passport needed in store; passport copy if refund > 100 UAH; bank card and cardholder name in Ukrainian.
- Where: any Rozetka store with a service desk, or Nova Poshta to "ТОВ «Термінал Розетка», сервіс", Kyiv, Kyrylivska 69. Sender pays, declared value = receipt price, cancel COD and "return delivery" services. In store, if no instant decision, item goes to expertise up to 14 days.
- Shipping: non-defective within 14 (30) days: customer pays, at carrier tariff. Wrong item / poor quality / not as pictured: Rozetka compensates the branch shipping, only after service confirms the defect. Claim: keep the shipping receipt, wait for the item decision, fill the compensation form, email form plus receipt photo to service@rozetka.com.ua.
- Refund: within 2-7 days after the decision (RozetkaEU: 14 calendar days after goods reach the warehouse and decision). Card payments go back to the same card. COD fee is not refunded.
- Defective flow: no separate defect or warranty flow on these pages; only the in-store expertise step. Not stated.
- Non-returnable: page says "not all categories", links to a separate article; no list on page.
- UX: numbered account steps; statuses not described. Long-form help articles; some pages old (389 and 1794 days per search snippet).

## Epicentr (epicentrk.ua)
Pages: https://epicentrk.ua/ua/info/return/ ; https://epicentrk.ua/ua/faq/povernennya-garantiya-servis/yak-zdiisnyty-povernennia-tovaru.html ; https://epicentrk.ua/ua/faq/povernennya-garantiya-servis/naskilky-shvydko-bude-zdiisneno-povernennia-koshtiv.html ; https://epicentrk.ua/ua/faq/povernennya-garantiya-servis/yak-zdiisnyty-povernennia-tovaru-protiahom-vstanovlenoho-stroku-harantii.html
- Period: 14 days, not counting the purchase day.
- Conditions: unused, seals and tags intact, complete set and packaging, warranty card and proof of payment kept, not on the Cabinet Resolution No. 172 list.
- Start, online order: (a) Pickup Center: bring item, warranty card, original receipt or card slip or invoice, passport; fill application template; staff check for signs of use, technically complex goods may need authorised-service diagnostics. (b) Nova Poshta: request in "Гарантія та сервіс" on site or Viber/Telegram bot > wait for service confirmation > pack with warranty card, receipt copy, completed application > send photo of the waybill with name, order number, waybill number, reason > inspection > refund. In-store purchases: "Повернення товару" cash desk with item, receipt, ID. Businesses: "Безготівковий розрахунок" desk, invoice.
- Shipping payer: not stated.
- Refund: Pickup Center / delivery service: same day as contract termination, or within 7 days if the till lacks funds, extendable to 30 days. Courier-paid: within 7 banking days via Ukrposhta/Nova Poshta branch with SMS transfer number. Website card payment: to same card within 7 banking days, up to 30 days.
- Defective flow: needs authorised service centre document stating significant defect, plus warranty card, receipt, full set. Pickup route: service act reviewed within 3 days. Courier route: document check 3-14 days. If document qualifies, refund; if not, item sent back. Repair/replacement terms and diagnostic time not stated.
- Non-returnable named on page: gift cards, alcohol/tobacco of proper quality, pharmacy medicines and medical products, plus Resolution No. 172 list.
- UX: page splits every path by channel (store vs online) and customer type (individual vs business). Return starts from a "Гарантія та сервіс" request, with a messenger bot as alternative to a form.

## Comfy
Pages: https://faq.comfy.ua/to-return-the-goods/ ; https://faq.comfy.ua/to-return-the-money/ ; also listed by search (snippets only, not opened): https://faq.comfy.ua/mp-return/ , https://faq.comfy.ua/receive-money/ .
- Period: 14 days, not counting the purchase day.
- Conditions: unused, presentation, seals and tags kept, receipt kept. Items from a service centre are returnable only with a written conclusion of a significant defect.
- Start: page does not say; it links to separate articles (prepare the item, where to go, what to do if defect found, how to get money). Contact: 0-800-303-505, 08:00-21:00 daily.
- Shipping payer: not stated on opened pages. Marketplace-return snippet says buyer pays when the item has no defect (SNIPPET).
- Refund: same method as payment. Cash at the till. Card up to 7 days depending on bank. A return invoice is created, then a receipt starting "RRN…" confirms the return. Credit purchases: 5 working days (SNIPPET). Marketplace: 3-5 bank days after seller posts refund (SNIPPET).
- Defect flow: not on opened pages.
- Non-returnable: Resolution No. 172, linked, not reproduced.
- UX: one narrow FAQ entry per question, sidebar of related return articles; the "RRN receipt" as proof of return is a concrete artefact. Customer reviews on vidhuk.ua report refusals for "signs of use": store staff judgement decides (secondary source, not official).

## Foxtrot
Pages: https://www.foxtrot.com.ua/uk/article/1400 (dated 11.10.2019) ; https://faq.foxtrot.com.ua/article/404627 . Refund page https://faq.foxtrot.com.ua/article/404634 returned HTTP 500: UNVERIFIED (snippet only: card refund usually up to 7 working days).
- Period: 14 days for site purchases, counted from receiving the sales document or goods.
- Conditions: original condition, completeness, consumer properties. Page says unpacking or testing alone does not mean refusal. FAQ snippet adds: full set, factory packaging, fiscal receipt (paper or electronic).
- Start: call 0 800 300 353 (09:00-20:00), chat, or contact form. Remote: photograph item and defect > message chat/form with reason > specialist approves and helps fill the application > pack in factory set > include application, warranty card, original receipt > send by Nova Poshta to the nearest Foxtrot store > specialist phones to confirm. Only a store employee makes the final decision, even after contact-centre pre-approval.
- Shipping payer: not stated.
- Refund: "terms set by law"; no number of days on opened pages.
- Defect flow: needs a service centre act describing the fault; without the act no exchange or refund (SNIPPET).
- Non-returnable: Resolution No. 172 only (SNIPPET).
- UX: photo-first step is worth noting; the main page is a thin 2019 article.

## 220volt.com.ua (tool retailer)
Page: https://220volt.com.ua/ua/information/uslugi/garanty/
- Period: 14 days after purchase; only new unused goods; full set, intact packaging with all parts, tags, factory markings.
- Start: tell a manager > fill application form > email form plus passport scan > get authorised service centre conclusion that operating conditions were not violated > come to the office with originals (passport, invoice, service conclusion). Return of a non-working item requires the service-centre conclusion.
- Shipping payer: not stated (links to a separate delivery page, not opened).
- Refund: no timing stated. Refund only if item is in full set including all packaging.
- Defect flow: warranty 1-15 years by manufacturer, shown on product page. Warranty card (manufacturer's or store's) with model, serial number, term, sale date. Exchange or refund only when service centre marks "significant defect" and the item is non-repairable. Service refusal grounds: broken seals, mechanical damage, ignoring the manual, unauthorised opening, wrong or altered warranty card/serial number. Not covered: natural wear, accident, mains faults, misuse, foreign objects, liquid, dust.
- Non-returnable: no categories listed.
- UX: the most tool-specific wording; return of tools is gated by a service verdict, and the office visit is hard for remote buyers.

## Makita Ukraine (manufacturer warranty)
Pages: https://www.makita.ua/warranty.html ; https://www.makita.ua/repair-service-process.html
- Warranty: 12 months for tool and its bundled battery/charger; 6 months for separately bought battery, charger, torch. Registering on the site within 4 weeks extends tools to 3 years and bundled batteries to 2 years.
- Documents: original correctly filled warranty card (model, serial, sale date, seller stamp and signature, buyer signature) and proof of purchase with matching date.
- Flow: bring to an authorised service centre, or via dealer who forwards it, or send by Nova Poshta to "Makita Ukraine", Brovary (batteries, chargers, torches are serviced only in Brovary). Service form (PDF) filled first. Makita chooses repair, exchange or replacement. Warranty service does not extend or restart the warranty.
- Shipping: for an accepted warranty case Makita Ukraine pays shipping and carries transport risk. For the dealer route the repair-process page does not say who pays.
- Diagnostics/repair time and statuses: not stated.
- Excluded: consumables (lubricants, rubber seals, filters, brushes, belts, hoses, nozzles), wear accessories (chucks, saw blades, cutting heads, drill bits), battery short circuits or non-original charger, self-opened or disassembled tools, overload, misuse, normal wear, transport/storage damage.
- UX: two numbered routes (direct, via dealer) with a downloadable request form.

## Patterns worth copying
- State 14 days and the counting rule in the first line; for online orders count from receipt of goods (Foxtrot does this).
- Say plainly who pays: customer for non-defective, shop for confirmed defect, with a reimbursement claim path (Rozetka: receipt + form + email). Most retailers leave it unstated, which invites disputes.
- Split the page by "item is fine" vs "item is defective", then by channel (store, Nova Poshta), as Epicentr does.
- Numbered Nova Poshta steps: request first, shop confirms, pack with documents, send waybill photo, track (Epicentr, Foxtrot). Prevents unannounced parcels.
- Ask for photos of the item and defect before shipping (Foxtrot); it filters cases cheaply.
- Give refund timelines as concrete numbers per payment method (Epicentr, Rozetka 2-7 days). Add a proof-of-return document (Comfy RRN receipt).
- For tools, tie the defect path to a service-centre verdict and say who pays diagnostics and shipping; Makita pays for accepted warranty cases. Avoid 220volt's office-visit requirement for remote buyers.
- List exclusions concretely (opened consumables such as discs, bits, blades; used batteries; installed items) rather than only linking the resolution; Makita's exclusion list is a model. Only the legal list binds, so the law agent must confirm what a shop may actually refuse.
